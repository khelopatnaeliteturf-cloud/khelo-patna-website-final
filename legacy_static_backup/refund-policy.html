Refund & Cancellation Policy

We aim to provide a smooth and fair booking experience for all our customers. Please review our refund and cancellation rules below:

1. Cancellations by Customers

Cancellations made 24 hours before slot time – eligible for full refund or rescheduling.

Cancellations made within 24 hours of slot time – non-refundable.

No-shows will not be refunded.

2. Cancellations by Management

If the turf is unavailable due to weather, technical issues, or safety concerns, customers will receive:

  • Either a 100% refund OR
  • Option to reschedule slot at no extra cost.

3. Academy Fees

Academy fees are non-refundable once a batch has started.

In exceptional cases (medical emergencies), requests may be considered.

4. Mode of Refund

Refunds will be credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

Thank you for understanding and supporting fair play at Khelo Patna Elite Turf.
