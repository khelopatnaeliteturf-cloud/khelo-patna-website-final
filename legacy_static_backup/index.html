From the Heart of SDPS... to the Soul of the City

Born out of the vision of S.D. Public School, KheloPatna Elite Turf aims to revolutionize the city's sports ecosystem. We're not just offering turf — we're offering a complete sports experience, powered by technology, guided by coaches, and built for champions.

With real-time booking, streaming-enabled turf, and coaching for every age — our goal is simple: Make sports accessible, fun, and elite for Patna.
